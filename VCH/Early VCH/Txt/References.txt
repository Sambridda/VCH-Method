Data Provenance & Reference Source (Metals)

The values provided below are not made up. They are derived from standard material property reference databases:

    NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology) Materials Data / Cryogenic Material Properties Database for sub-zero temperature thermal property estimations.

    ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code (Section II, Part D) and ASM Handbook, Vol. 1 & 2 for thermal properties of Stainless Steel (AISI 304 grade standard) and Mild Steel (Low-carbon steel, ~0.2% C) from room temperature up to 200°C.

Reference Sources & Standards (Insulation)

    ASTM International Standards: Specific consensus testing methods (such as ASTM C177 / ASTM C518 guarded hot plate method) and material specifications linked in the table.

    ASHRAE Handbook of Fundamentals: Contains tabulated thermophysical properties for thermal insulation materials under steady-state condition.

    Perry's Chemical Engineers' Handbook (Section 11: Heat Transfer Equipment): Standard reference for process tank insulation properties.
